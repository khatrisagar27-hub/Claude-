## Razorpay Setup

1. Create account at razorpay.com (KYC with CA firm's PAN + bank account)
2. Go to Invoices → Create Invoice Template
3. Add your firm details: Name, Address, GSTIN, bank account
4. SAC code for CA services: **998211** (Accounting and Auditing services)
5. GST rate: 18%
6. Create invoice for each client milestone — email directly from Razorpay
7. Clients can pay via UPI, NEFT, IMPS, or card — you get notified instantly

**Advantage over manual invoicing:** Automatic payment tracking, professional appearance, instant payment confirmation.

## Google Drive Client Structure

Create one folder per client. Structure:

```
/Clients
  /[Client Name] - [Year of Engagement Start]
    /Documents Received
      /FY 2024-25
      /FY 2025-26
    /Returns Filed
      /FY 2024-25
      /FY 2025-26
    /Correspondence
      /Notices
      /Replies
    /Engagement
      /Engagement Letter (signed)
      /Audit Report
      /Annual Summary
```

Share the "Documents Received" subfolder with the client — they can upload directly, no email attachments.

## WhatsApp Business Setup

1. Download WhatsApp Business
2. Set up with your firm's number
3. Create a Business Profile: CA Firm Name, website (if any), email, hours
4. Use this for:
   - Document reminders: "Advance tax deadline is 15 Sep — please share updated income estimate"
   - Quick queries from clients
   - Sending payment links (Razorpay generates a shareable payment link)
5. Do NOT use for cold outreach or promotion — only for active clients and warm contacts
