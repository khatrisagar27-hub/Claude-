# Referral Program

**When to launch:** After your first paying Sprint client completes their engagement  
**Incentive:** 1 free retainer month ($1,500 value) per referred client who pays  
**Why this over cash commissions:** Higher perceived value, protects your margins, feels generous without a payout

---

## The Referral Ask (Send at End of Sprint)

Send via email or LinkedIn DM within 24 hours of the final session.

```
Subject: Working with you has been great + a quick note

Hey [First Name],

Working with you over the past 30 days has been genuinely great.

[One specific thing you're proud of that you achieved together or observed about them]

I'm looking to work with 2–3 more founders like you over the next 60 days.

If you know someone who's dealing with [the specific problem you solved for them], 
and you introduce us — if they become a client, I'll give you one full month 
of retainer access at no charge ($1,500 value).

No pressure at all. I'm not looking for a formal referral arrangement — 
just thought you should know the door is open.

If someone comes to mind: [their name] + [brief intro about why they'd benefit] 
is all I need. I'll take it from there.

Thank you again for the trust. It mattered.

[Your Name]
```

---

## Referral Tracking

Add a column to your pipeline tracker: **Referred By**

When someone books a call via referral:
- [ ] Note the referrer's name in the tracker
- [ ] Mention the referrer early in the discovery call ("[Name] mentioned you might find this useful")
- [ ] If they close: immediately send the referrer their free retainer month via a Stripe link or just schedule it on Calendly

---

## Why Referrals Beat Advertising at This Stage

| Channel | Cost | Conversion Rate | Avg. Time to Close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold LinkedIn DMs | $0 | 20–25% close rate on calls | 2–4 weeks |
| Referral | $1,500 (in-kind) | 50–70% close rate on calls | 3–7 days |
| Paid Ads | $500–2,000/month | 5–15% close rate on calls | 4–8 weeks |

**A single referral at a 60% close rate saves you 20–30 cold DMs and 2–3 weeks.**

---

## Referral Flywheel (Month 3+)

By Month 3, you should have 3–5 completed Sprint clients.

Each client asked for a referral = potential 1–3 new inbound leads without any outreach.

**The flywheel:**

```
Sprint Client → Referral Ask → New Lead Booked
    ↓                                ↓
 Retainer Upsell            New Sprint Client
    ↓                                ↓
More Proof / Testimonials    More Referral Asks
    ↓
 Reduced Cold Outreach Need
```

**Target for Month 4+:** 50% of new clients come from referrals. That cuts your DM time from 2 hrs/week to under 1 hr/week.

## Testimonial Collection (Pairs With Referral Ask)

Ask for a written testimonial in the same email or a separate message.

```
One more thing — if you'd be willing to share a line or two about 
your experience working together, it would mean a lot.

Specifically helpful: what you were dealing with before, and 
what's different now. Even 2–3 sentences is plenty.

You can reply here or send it to [your email]. 

No pressure at all — if the timing isn't right, I completely understand.
```

**Use format for social proof:**  
> "[Before state]. After working with [Your Name], [specific outcome]. [Optional: what made it different]."  
> — [First Name L.], [Title], [Company type — vague if they prefer privacy]

**Where to use testimonials:**
- LinkedIn (as a native post, not a screenshot)
- Landing page social proof section
- Email signature after Month 2
- Discovery call ("a recent client said...")
