# Retainer Upgrade System

**When to introduce:** At Session 2 of every Sprint (mid-engagement, peak excitement)  
**Price:** $1,500/month  
**Your time:** 1.5–2 hours/month per client  
**Revenue impact:** 3 retainer clients = $4,500/month in passive-ish income that reduces new client pressure

---

## The Retainer Offer

**Name:** The Strategy Retainer  
**Price:** $1,500/month  
**What's included:**
- 1 x 60-minute monthly strategy call
- Unlimited async questions via email (48-hour response SLA)
- Monthly strategic review document (1-page progress snapshot + priority adjustment)

**Your actual time per client:** 1.5–2 hours/month  
**Hourly equivalent:** $750–$1,000/hour

---

## When to Pitch (Exact Timing)

**Pitch at Session 2, not Session 3.**

Why: Session 2 is peak trust + peak excitement. They've seen your thinking, they're partway through the roadmap, and they're starting to see what's possible. By Session 3, the engagement feels like it's ending — urgency drops.

**The transition moment in Session 2:**

Once you've done the roadmap review and they're reacting positively (nodding, asking execution questions, saying "this is exactly what we needed") — pause and say:

*"Before we wrap today, I want to flag something.*

*You're now 2 weeks into a 30-day engagement. You have the roadmap. You've got clarity on the priorities.*

*The question a lot of founders ask at this point is: 'What happens after the 30 days?' Because execution doesn't stop — the questions keep coming.*

*I offer a retainer for clients who want ongoing access. One call a month, async anytime, and a monthly review of where you are relative to the roadmap. It's $1,500 a month.*

*Some founders don't need it — the roadmap is enough. Others want a thinking partner in their corner as they execute. Which feels more like you?"*

**Stop there. Let them answer.**

## Objection Handling

**"I'll see how things go after the Sprint first."**  
*"Fair. I'll send you a note at the end of our final session with the option. Just so you know — I typically have a waitlist for retainer spots, so if you decide you want one, reach out sooner rather than later."*

(This is true by Month 2 if you're growing as planned. If it's not true yet, make the waitlist real by capping retainer spots at 5.)

**"$1,500 feels like a lot for one call a month."**  
*"The call is 60 minutes. But the value isn't in the call — it's in the async access between calls. Most clients message me 3–5 times a month with decisions, blockers, or gut-check questions. That access is what moves them faster. The call is just the anchor."*

**"Can I do it every other month instead?"**  
*"I don't offer a bimonthly structure — the continuity between calls is part of how it works. But if budget is the constraint, I can start you on a 3-month minimum and you can cancel after that with 30 days notice."*

## Retainer Onboarding (After They Say Yes)

1. Send Stripe recurring payment link (set to auto-charge monthly)
2. Send a 1-page onboarding doc:

```
Welcome to the Strategy Retainer.

Here's how this works:

Monthly call: I'll send a Calendly link each month. Book anytime in the last 
2 weeks of the month. Bring your 3 biggest current priorities or decisions.

Async questions: Email me anytime. I respond within 48 hours on business days.
Use the subject line [ASYNC] so I prioritize it.

Monthly review: On the 1st of each month, I'll send you a 1-page snapshot 
of progress vs. your roadmap + suggested priority adjustments.

One rule: Send me your monthly update before our call (template below). 
It makes our 60 minutes 3x more useful.
```

3. Send the monthly update template:

```
Monthly Update Template

1. Biggest win since last call:
2. Biggest challenge right now:
3. Top 3 decisions I need input on:
4. What's blocking my #1 priority:
5. Anything I want to revisit from the roadmap:
```

## When to Stop Taking New Sprint Clients

When retainer clients reach 7+, your time (1–5 hrs/week) becomes constrained. At that point:

1. Raise Sprint price to $5,000 (natural demand filter)
2. Cap retainers at 8–10 clients
3. Consider building a group program or online course as leverage — you now have 10+ case studies and a proven system
