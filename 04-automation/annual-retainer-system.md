# Annual Retainer System

Converting every client to an annual engagement is the core of reaching $10K/month — it means your revenue compounds year over year without requiring new clients every month.

---

## The Renewal Conversation

The best time to confirm the next year's engagement: **February**, during the year-end review call.

The client is receiving their final tax computation, seeing the full year's planning in action, and feeling the outcome. This is peak satisfaction — the right moment to confirm continuation.

**In the February review call:**

*"We're wrapping up FY [XX]. Before I finalize the return, I want to flag that [X] changed this year that will affect next year's planning — specifically [brief mention]. The Finance Act may bring further changes in the Budget next month.*

*I'd like to continue managing your planning for FY [next year]. Given [any increased complexity / scope changes], my fee for next year will be ₹[X]. If you'd like to confirm now, I can start pre-filing preparation in April rather than waiting."*

**Why this works:**
- February timing = client is actively engaged with tax matters
- You're framing the value of continuity, not asking for a renewal
- Proposing a concrete next step (April preparation) makes it easy to say yes

---

## Year-Over-Year Fee Escalation

Raise fees 10–15% annually. How to communicate it:

*"Tax complexity has increased this year — the new [amendment/regime changes/reporting requirements] adds to the work involved. My fee for FY [XX] will be ₹[new fee]. This reflects the expanded scope."*

Clients who are receiving genuine value do not push back on 10–15% increases. Clients who push back are telling you they don't see the value — which is information you need.

## Value-Add Touchpoints Through the Year

These are not "check-ins" — they are genuine deliverables that remind the client why they pay you. Deliver these proactively, without being asked.

| Month | Touchpoint | Time Required |
|---|---|---|
| April | "FY Start Planning Note" — new year structure confirmation | 30 min |
| May–June | Advance tax estimate + payment advisory | 45 min per client |
| July | Budget impact summary (post-Union Budget) | 1 hour per client |
| September | Mid-year income review + Q2 advance tax | 30 min per client |
| October | Investment planning reminder (80C, 80D deadline approaching) | 15 min per client |
| December | Q3 advance tax + year-end projection | 30 min per client |
| February | Final computation + year-end review call | 60 min per client |
| March | ITR filing + Q4 advance tax | 45 min per client |

**Total time per client per year:** 8–12 hours  
**Your fee per client:** ₹1,50,000–₹3,00,000  
**Effective hourly rate:** ₹12,500–₹37,500/hour

This is the correct metric to track — not hours worked, but value per hour.

## Expanding Existing Client Relationships

Each annual client is a potential multi-service relationship. After the first year:

**Year 1:** Personal income tax planning for the director  
**Year 2 add-on:** HUF creation and planning (if applicable)  
**Year 2 add-on:** Spouse's income planning (if separate income)  
**Year 3 add-on:** Company tax structuring advisory  
**Year 3 add-on:** Children's investment / income planning (if adults)  
**Ongoing:** Capital gains transactions as they occur

A single ₹50 crore business owner relationship can reasonably generate ₹3–5 lakh annually across personal planning, HUF, family income, and business advisory — over time.

## Client Retention Factors

Clients who leave do so because:
1. **They don't feel the value** — they paid but didn't see outcomes explained clearly. Fix: always send a year-end summary showing what was done and the approximate tax impact.
2. **They found someone cheaper** — the cheaper CA typically does less. Fix: ensure your fee is justified by visible, specific outcomes every year.
3. **A family member/friend CA took over** — this happens. Not much you can do. Focus on retention through relationship depth, not just technical quality.

**Year-end summary format (send with final computation):**

```
FY [XX] Tax Planning Summary — [Client Name]

What we did this year:
1. Restructured remuneration: [X] — tax impact: ~₹[Y]
2. Optimized deductions [80C, 80D, etc.]: [X] — tax impact: ~₹[Y]
3. Advance tax management: Paid ₹[X] across 4 installments — interest saved: ~₹[Y]
4. [Any other specific action]

Total estimated tax saving vs. unplanned baseline: ₹[approximate]

For FY [next year]:
- [1–2 forward-looking actions planned]
- Watch for: [Budget change / regime shift / personal income change]

We look forward to continuing this engagement.
```

This document makes the value tangible. Clients who receive it never question the fee.
