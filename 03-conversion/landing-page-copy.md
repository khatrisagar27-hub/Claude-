## A/B Test Priority (After First 10 Calls)

1. **Headline:** Test specificity of outcome (generic vs. specific number)
2. **CTA button text:** "Apply" vs. "Book" vs. "See If You Qualify"
3. **Guarantee placement:** Above vs. below the CTA

Don't test anything until you have 10+ discovery calls booked. Data before optimization.
