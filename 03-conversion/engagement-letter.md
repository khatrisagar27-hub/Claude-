# Engagement Letter Templates

Every client relationship must begin with a signed engagement letter. This protects both parties, sets scope expectations, and is a professional requirement.

---

## Template A — Tax Planning Audit (One-Time)

```
[CA FIRM LETTERHEAD]
[Date]

To,
[Client Name]
[Designation]
[Company Name]
[Address]

Dear [Client Name],

ENGAGEMENT LETTER — TAX PLANNING AUDIT

We are pleased to confirm our engagement to conduct a Tax Planning Audit 
as discussed. This letter sets out the terms of our engagement.

SCOPE OF SERVICES

We will review:
a) Individual Income Tax Returns for FY [XX] and FY [XX]
b) Director remuneration structure and Form 16
c) Business financial statements (P&L and Balance Sheet) for FY [XX]
d) Advance tax challans paid during the relevant periods
e) Investment and deduction documentation (if provided)

We will deliver a written Tax Planning Audit Report identifying:
- Current tax structure and effective tax rate
- Legitimate optimization opportunities under the Income Tax Act, 1961
- Recommended income restructuring options
- Projected impact of each recommendation (approximate)
- Priority of implementation

SCOPE EXCLUSIONS

This engagement does not include:
- Implementation of recommendations
- Filing of returns or revised returns
- Representation before tax authorities
- GST or other indirect tax review (can be scoped separately)

CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

You agree to provide the following within [10] working days of this 
letter being countersigned:
[Document checklist to be shared separately]

Timely and accurate document submission is required for us to complete 
the engagement within the agreed timeline.

PROFESSIONAL FEES

Our professional fee for this engagement is ₹[X,XX,000] + GST @ 18%.

Payment terms:
- 50% (₹[X]) on countersigning of this letter
- 50% (₹[X]) on delivery of the Audit Report

Invoices will be raised from [CA Firm Name], GSTIN: [GSTIN].

TIMELINE

We will deliver the Audit Report within [15] working days of receipt of 
all requested documents.

CONFIDENTIALITY

All information shared with us in the course of this engagement will be 
treated as strictly confidential and will not be disclosed to any third 
party except as required by law or professional standards.

GOVERNING STANDARDS

This engagement will be conducted in accordance with the Institute of 
Chartered Accountants of India's Standards on Related Services and the 
ICAI Code of Ethics.

Please countersign and return a copy of this letter to confirm your 
agreement to these terms.

Yours faithfully,

[Your Name]
Chartered Accountant
Membership No.: [ICAI No.]
[CA Firm Name]
[Date]


ACCEPTED AND AGREED:

Name: _________________________
Designation: _________________________
Date: _________________________
Signature: _________________________
```

## Template B — Annual Tax Planning Engagement

```
[CA FIRM LETTERHEAD]
[Date]

To,
[Client Name]
[Designation]
[Company Name]
[Address]

Dear [Client Name],

ENGAGEMENT LETTER — ANNUAL TAX PLANNING ENGAGEMENT
FY [YEAR-YEAR]

SCOPE OF SERVICES

For the financial year [FY XX-XX], we will provide the following services:

1. TAX PLANNING AND STRUCTURING
   - Review and recommendation of optimal remuneration structure
   - Income head classification and optimization
   - Deduction planning under relevant sections of the Income Tax Act
   - HUF / family tax planning advisory (if applicable)

2. ADVANCE TAX MANAGEMENT
   - Advance tax computation for each installment (June, September, December, March)
   - Computation and payment advisory
   - Review and adjustment based on actual income through the year

3. QUARTERLY REVIEWS
   - Four review interactions (one per quarter) to assess income vs. estimate
   - Adjustment recommendations as circumstances change

4. YEAR-END COMPLIANCE
   - Final tax computation
   - Filing of Income Tax Return (ITR) — [ITR form to be specified]
   - Form 16 review and reconciliation

SCOPE EXCLUSIONS

This engagement does not include:
- Company/business entity income tax returns [unless separately agreed]
- GST returns or advisory
- Tax audit under Section 44AB
- Representation before assessing officers or appellate authorities
- Any services not listed above

PROFESSIONAL FEES

Our professional fee for this annual engagement is ₹[X,XX,000] + GST @ 18%.

Payment schedule:
- ₹[X] (50%) — Due by [April 30, FY start]
- ₹[X] (25%) — Due by [October 15, mid-year]
- ₹[X] (25%) — Due by [February 28, year-end]

Invoices will be raised at each payment milestone.

TERM AND RENEWAL

This engagement covers FY [XX-XX] (April 1, [year] to March 31, [year]).

It will be renewed annually, subject to mutual agreement on fees for the 
subsequent year. We will send renewal terms by [February 15] each year.

Either party may terminate this engagement with [30] days' written notice. 
Fees for work completed up to the date of termination will be due and payable.

DOCUMENT SUBMISSION

You agree to share the following by [April 30] each year:
- Form 16 from the company
- Investment declarations / proofs
- Details of income from other sources (rental, interest, capital gains)
- Any notices received from the Income Tax Department

CLIENT COOPERATION

Timely decision-making and document sharing are necessary for this 
engagement to be effective. We will not be responsible for outcomes 
resulting from delayed or incomplete information.

CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

All client information is held in strict confidence in accordance with 
the ICAI Code of Ethics and applicable data protection norms.

We look forward to a productive engagement.

Yours faithfully,

[Your Name]
Chartered Accountant
Membership No.: [ICAI No.]
[CA Firm Name]
[Date]


ACCEPTED AND AGREED:

Name: _________________________
Designation: _________________________
PAN: _________________________
Date: _________________________
Signature: _________________________
```

## Document Checklist (Share With Every New Client)

```
TAX PLANNING AUDIT / ANNUAL ENGAGEMENT — DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Please provide the following for FY [XX-XX] and FY [XX-XX]:

PERSONAL INCOME
[ ] ITR acknowledgement (ITR-V) for last 2 years
[ ] Form 16 from employer/company (Parts A and B)
[ ] Bank statements (savings + current) — full year
[ ] Interest certificates (FD, savings account, PPF)
[ ] Rental income details (agreement + rent receipts if applicable)
[ ] Capital gains statements (broker statements, property sale documents)
[ ] Agricultural income details (if any)

BUSINESS INCOME (if proprietorship or partnership)
[ ] P&L and Balance Sheet for last 2 years
[ ] ITR-3 / ITR-4 filing acknowledgement

DEDUCTIONS AND INVESTMENTS
[ ] 80C investment proofs (LIC, PPF, ELSS, home loan principal, etc.)
[ ] 80D health insurance premium receipts
[ ] Home loan interest certificate (Section 24)
[ ] Donation receipts (80G) if applicable
[ ] NPS contribution statement (80CCD)

ADVANCE TAX
[ ] Advance tax challan copies (all 4 installments) for last 2 years
[ ] Self-assessment tax challans (if any)

OTHER
[ ] PAN card copy
[ ] Aadhaar card copy
[ ] Any notices received from Income Tax Department
[ ] Details of HUF (if existing)
[ ] Details of investments in spouse's / children's names (for clubbing consideration)

Please share documents via email or secure file transfer. Physical copies 
can be submitted at our office.
```
