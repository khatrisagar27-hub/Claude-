# Email Sequences

Set these up in MailerLite as triggered sequences. Customize every `[bracket]` before activating.

---

## Sequence A — Post-Discovery Call (Non-Closer)

Triggered: Manually tag the lead in MailerLite after the call ends without a close.  
Send from: Your personal email address (not a brand domain — personal converts better at this stage)

---

### Email 1 — Day 0 (Same Evening as Call)

**Subject:** Notes from our call + next step

```
Hey [First Name],

Really enjoyed our conversation today.

Here's what I took away from what you shared:

You're dealing with [their problem in their own words — copy from your call notes].

You've already tried [what they said they tried].

And if this isn't resolved in the next 6 months, the cost to you is 
[their exact Q3 answer — repeat their words, not yours].

The Strategy Sprint addresses exactly this. Specifically, we'd start with 
[first session deliverable] so you immediately have clarity on [the specific 
bottleneck you diagnosed on the call].

What's included:
→ 3 x 60-minute working sessions
→ Custom 90-day strategic roadmap
→ 30 days async support (email/Slack)

Investment: $2,500

If you're ready to move forward: [Stripe payment link]

If you have questions — hit reply. I read every email.

[Your Name]
```

**Rules:**
- Do not mention a deadline in Email 1
- Do not add social proof links or case studies
- Keep it under 200 words — shorter reads as more confident
- Use their exact words from the call — this shows you listened

---

### Email 2 — Day 3

**Subject:** One thing I forgot to mention

```
Hey [First Name],

I realized I didn't mention this on our call:

Every Strategy Sprint comes with a full guarantee.

If after our first working session together you don't feel this is going 
to deliver [specific outcome you discussed], I'll refund you 100%. 
No questions asked.

I've never had anyone take me up on it — but I want you to have zero risk 
in saying yes.

[Stripe payment link] — I have one spot remaining this month.

[Your Name]
```

**Why this works:** The guarantee removes the last objection for anyone who's been sitting on the fence. Short email = high read rate. One link = no decision paralysis.

---

### Email 3 — Day 7

**Subject:** Closing the loop

```
Hey [First Name],

Following up one last time — I don't want to keep showing up in your inbox 
if the timing isn't right.

I do have one spot open right now. After this week, I'm fully booked through [Month].

If you're ready: [Stripe payment link]

If the timing isn't right — no problem at all.

Either way, here's something that might be useful regardless:

[One genuinely useful, actionable insight specific to the problem they described. 
2–3 sentences. Give real value, not a teaser.]

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Rules:**
- "Closing the loop" subject line has unusually high open rates — keep it
- The free insight at the end is not a tease — it's real value. People remember who gave them something useful even when they didn't buy.
- After Email 3, stop the sequence. Move them to a "nurture" folder and check back manually in 45–60 days.

## Sequence B — Lead Magnet Welcome Sequence

Triggered: Automatically when someone submits the Tally.so scorecard form.  
Purpose: Warm up cold leads who downloaded the scorecard but haven't booked a call.

---

### Welcome Email — Immediate (Auto-Send via MailerLite)

**Subject:** Your Strategy Scorecard is ready

```
Hey [First Name],

Your [Niche] Strategy Scorecard is attached / linked here:

[Google Drive PDF link — set to "Anyone with link can view"]

Run through the 10 questions honestly. The score at the end will tell you 
exactly where your biggest leverage point is.

If you score under 35 — we should talk.

I work with [ICP type] founders to [specific outcome] in 30 days.
The Sprint is what the scorecard points to.

Here's my calendar if you want to explore it:
[Calendly link]

No pitch — just 20 minutes of honest diagnosis.

[Your Name]
```

---

### Follow-Up Email — Day 3

**Subject:** Did you take the scorecard?

```
Hey [First Name],

Checking in — did you get a chance to run through the scorecard?

Most [ICP type] founders who take it score between 20–35.

The most common low-score area? [The specific category from your scorecard 
where most people struggle — based on your experience.]

That's the one that tends to compound the fastest if left uncorrected.

If you want to talk through what your score means for your specific situation:
[Calendly link]

20 minutes. I'll tell you honestly what I see.

[Your Name]
```

---

### Follow-Up Email — Day 7

**Subject:** The one thing the scorecard doesn't tell you

```
Hey [First Name],

The scorecard shows WHERE the bottleneck is.

It doesn't show WHY — that's what the 20-minute call is for.

In my experience, [one insight about the non-obvious root cause behind the 
most common low-score area]. 

Most founders try to fix the symptom. The ones who move fastest fix the cause.

If you're in that category:
[Calendly link]

[Your Name]
```

After Day 7, stop automated emails. If they haven't booked, move them to a monthly newsletter segment and check back when you have a new insight, case study, or offer to share.

---

## MailerLite Setup Instructions

1. Sign up at mailerlite.com (free up to 1,000 subscribers)
2. Create two Groups: "Sprint Leads" and "Scorecard Downloads"
3. Create Sequence A (label: "Post-Call Follow-Up") — set to Manual Trigger
4. Create Sequence B (label: "Scorecard Welcome") — set to Auto-Trigger on Group join
5. In Tally.so → Integrations → MailerLite: map email field, add to "Scorecard Downloads" group
6. After each call, manually add the lead to "Sprint Leads" group to trigger Sequence A

**Total setup time:** 2–3 hours one-time
