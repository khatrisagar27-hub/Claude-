⚠ Guardrails — read this first.
  • Confidentiality (ICAI Code of Ethics). Never paste a client's PII, price-sensitive or UDIN-linked data into a consumer chatbot. Anonymise — mask PAN/GSTIN/Aadhaar, replace names with roles. For sensitive work, use an enterprise / no-training deployment.
  • AI can hallucinate. It may invent a section number, circular, case citation or due date. Verify every statutory reference, rate, threshold and date against the bare Act, the latest Finance Act, CBDT/CBIC circulars or ICAI material before relying on it.
  • Judgement is not outsourced. Claude drafts and reasons; the CA reviews, applies professional skepticism and signs. Opinions, certifications, UDINs and filings remain your responsibility.
  • Law changes yearly. State the assessment/financial year and re-verify — the model has a knowledge cut-off.

Prompt anatomy for CAs — the 5-part formula

1. Role ("You are a CA in India specialising in…") · 2. Context (entity, turnover, period, the figures) · 3. Task (exactly what to produce) · 4. Format (table / memo / letter; length) · 5. Constraints (Indian law, cite sections, flag assumptions, no fabricated citations).

Keep this in your back pocket — paste at the end of any prompt:
"Cite exact Indian sections/forms; clearly mark anything you are assuming; if you are not certain a case/citation exists, say so rather than inventing it; ask me for any figure you need instead of guessing."

Ways of working — beyond single prompts

Ways of working

1 · Recurring monthly workflows

Chain saved prompts for the work you do every month: Monthly close → TB review → MIS commentary (G1) → variance (G3) → board narrative (G4). GST routine → reconciliation (C1) → exception review → notice replies (C3). Audit file review → CAE exceptions → review notes (A2) → management letter (A3).

2 · Work with spreadsheets & documents, not just text

Paste CSV/ledger extracts and ask Claude to reason over them. Keep the authoritative calculations in your own tools — the CAE for audit analytics, LexComply for applicability, the forex workbook for currency — and use Claude to interpret and write up the results. AI complements the signed work; it does not replace it.

3 · Build a house-style snippet bank

Save your firm's best prompts (engagement letter, MIS, notice reply) with your tone and standard clauses baked in. Over time this becomes a team asset that makes every article's first draft consistent.

Two habits that matter most: end every high-stakes prompt with the verification instruction above, and anonymise by default — mask PAN/GSTIN/Aadhaar and replace names with roles.
