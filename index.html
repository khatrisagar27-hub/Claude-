Profile

Sagar Khatri & Associates is a firm of Chartered Accountants established in 2022, founded and led by its proprietor, CA Sagar Khatri. Based in Vadodara, Gujarat, the firm serves individuals, professionals and small and medium enterprises with audit and assurance, direct and indirect taxation, and financial advisory.

Our work is grounded in accurate compliance, timely delivery and sound, practical financial advice.

The firm conducts its practice in accordance with the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and the standards and guidelines issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

Frequently asked questions

General information on common queries.

What services does the firm provide?

The firm provides audit and assurance, direct and indirect taxation (including GST), accounting, company-law/ROC compliance and financial advisory services. See the Services section above.

Who can engage the firm?

We work with individuals, professionals, and small and medium enterprises. You are welcome to reach us using the details in the Contact section.

What documents are needed to file an income-tax return?

Typically PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 / salary details, bank interest statements, capital-gains statements (if any), and details of deductions claimed. Requirements vary by case.

How can I get in touch?

Please use the office address, email, phone or the enquiry form in the Contact section below.
