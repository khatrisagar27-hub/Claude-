# Annual Tax Planning Engagement — Service Structure

## Your Unfair Advantage

You helped a ₹50 crore manufacturing director save ~30% in taxes through income restructuring and planned deductions. That is not a minor outcome — for a business at that scale, 30% tax saving can mean ₹15–40 lakh returned to the owner annually. Your fee at ₹2 lakh is a 7–20x return on investment for the client.

This is the frame for every conversation you have.

## Service Tiers

### Tier 1 — Tax Planning Audit (Entry Point)
**Fee:** ₹25,000–₹50,000 one-time  
**What you do:** Review last 2 years of ITRs, business financials, salary structure. Identify all missed deductions and restructuring opportunities. Deliver a written Tax Optimization Report.  
**Time:** 4–6 hours  
**Purpose:** This is the gateway — most clients who see the report immediately want you to implement it

### Tier 2 — Annual Tax Planning Engagement
**Fee:** ₹1,50,000–₹3,00,000/year  
**What you do:**
- Advance tax planning at the start of the financial year
- Salary/remuneration restructuring
- Planned deduction calendar (80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, etc.)
- Business expense planning (allowable deductions under business income)
- Investment-linked tax planning
- Quarterly review calls (4 per year)
- Year-end tax computation and filing
**Time:** 10–14 hours/year per client  
**Who this is for:** Business owners/directors, ₹25–200 crore turnover, paying ₹10 lakh+ in advance tax

### Tier 3 — Comprehensive Business + Personal Tax
**Fee:** ₹3,00,000–₹5,00,000/year  
**What you do:** Everything in Tier 2 plus GST advisory, business structure optimization (HUF, LLP, company), succession planning aspects  
**Time:** 20–25 hours/year per client  
**Who this is for:** Group companies, family-managed businesses, ₹100 crore+ turnover

## The Business Case (What You Tell Every Prospect)

Never say "I can save you X% in taxes." (Prohibited — specific outcome claim in solicitation context.)

Instead, in a professional conversation:

*"For a business at your turnover level, most owners are overpaying income tax by 20–35% simply because their income structure hasn't been reviewed. The tax code has legitimate provisions — salary optimization, allowances, HUF structures, deductions — that most people miss because no one's sat down and mapped their specific situation. That's what I do."*

Then let them ask the follow-up question.

## Initial Consultation Structure (Free, 45 Minutes)

This is not a sales call. It is a professional courtesy — and it naturally converts because you demonstrate competence.

**Part 1 — Understand (15 min):**
- What's their business structure? (Pvt Ltd, LLP, proprietorship, partnership)
- What's their personal income? (Director remuneration, dividends, rental, capital gains)
- What do they currently pay in advance tax / last year's total tax outgo?
- Are they using any tax planning currently?

**Part 2 — Diagnose (15 min):**
Based on what they share, identify 2–3 specific, legitimate opportunities they're missing. Be specific — mention the section, the provision, and roughly what it might mean for them.

Example:
*"At your remuneration structure, if you're not using the HRA and LTA components properly, you're likely leaving ₹2–4 lakh of deductible income on the table every year. And if the company isn't structured to route certain expenses through the business correctly under Section 37, that's another gap to look at."*

**Part 3 — Next Step (15 min):**
*"Based on what you've shared, there are several areas worth examining in detail. The right starting point is a proper Tax Planning Audit — I review your last 2 years of returns, your business P&L, and your personal income structure, and I give you a written report of every legitimate optimization available to you. From there, you decide whether you want to implement it yourself or have me manage it ongoing."*

## What You Do NOT Promise

- Do not promise a specific percentage of savings in writing or in initial conversations
- Do not guarantee tax outcomes (tax laws change; income changes)
- Do not promise to "save" taxes — frame as "optimize" or "plan legitimately"

**Safe framing:** "Based on situations similar to yours, there are typically significant opportunities — but the exact number depends on a thorough review of your specific structure."
