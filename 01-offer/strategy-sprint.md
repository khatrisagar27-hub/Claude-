## Pricing Conversation Script

When they ask "what does this cost?":

1. Pause. Don't rush to answer.
2. "Before I answer that — you mentioned this problem is costing you [their own number from earlier in the call]. Solving it is worth [their answer to 'what would solving it be worth']. With that context — the Sprint is $2,500. That includes [3 bullets]. Based on the math you just gave me, it's a straightforward decision. Want to move forward?"
3. Stop talking. Wait for their response.

## When to Raise Your Price

- After your **second** paying client: You have proof. Confidence is worth $500.
- When you have **2 people waiting**: Raise to $3,500. Demand signal = pricing power.
- When you're **declining clients**: Raise to $5,000 and position it as the premium tier.
