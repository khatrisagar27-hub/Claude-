# LinkedIn Cold Outreach Sequences

All templates use `[brackets]` for fill-ins. Personalize every DM before sending — even one sentence of genuine observation dramatically increases reply rates.

---

## Sequence A — Standard Cold (No Prior Signal)

Use for: Anyone in your ICP who hasn't posted about your specific topic recently.

### Step 1 — Connection Request

Send with no note, OR use this (under 200 characters):

```
Hi [First Name], I work with [their industry] founders on [specific outcome]. Saw your profile — thought it worth connecting.
```

**Do NOT pitch in the connection request. Ever.**

---

### Step 2 — First DM (Send 24–48 hours after they accept)

```
Hey [First Name] — thanks for connecting.

I noticed [one specific thing from their profile OR a recent post — 1 sentence. 
Examples: "you mentioned scaling the team to 40 last year" / "saw your post about the Q3 
ops bottleneck" / "your background in [X] is unusual — most [role] skip that entirely"].

I work with [company type] founders to [specific outcome you deliver].

Recently helped a [similar company type] go from [before state] to [after state] 
in [timeframe]. [Keep vague — no client names without permission.]

Not pitching anything — just curious: is [the problem you solve] something you're 
actively working on right now?
```

**Character count target:** Under 200 words. End with one yes/no question only.

---

### Step 3 — Follow-Up (Day 5, if no reply)

```
Hey [First Name] — following up in case this got buried.

I put together a free [lead magnet name — e.g. "Strategy Scorecard for SaaS Founders"] 
that covers [what it solves in one line].

Happy to send it over — no strings attached. Worth it?
```

**Do not apologize for following up. This reads as confident, not pushy.**

---

### Step 4 — Final Follow-Up (Day 10, if still no reply)

```
[First Name] — last message from me.

If [specific problem] ever becomes a priority, I'd be glad to connect.

One thing that might help regardless: [one genuinely useful insight or tip specific 
to their situation — 1–2 sentences]. 

[Link to lead magnet if relevant]

Take care.
```

**This is your exit. After this, move on. No fourth message.**

## Sequence B — Warm Signal (They Posted About Your Topic)

Use for: ICP targets who have recently posted about a problem you solve.

### Step 1 — Connection Request

```
Hi [First Name] — saw your post about [their topic]. Relevant to what I do 
with [their type of company]. Worth connecting.
```

### Step 2 — First DM (24 hours after acceptance)

```
Hey [First Name] — your post about [specific post topic] hit close to home.

That's exactly the problem I help [company type] founders solve. 

I recently worked with a [similar company] that was in the same spot — 
[before state]. We rebuilt [the thing] and got them to [after state] in [timeframe].

The approach isn't obvious — most people [common wrong move they're probably making].

Would it be useful if I sent over [lead magnet / free resource related to their post]?
```

**This sequence closes ~2x faster than Sequence A because you're entering an active conversation.**

## Sequence C — Referral-Triggered (Someone Mentioned You)

Use for: When a current or past client sends you a warm intro.

### Step 1 — DM immediately (same day as intro)

```
Hey [First Name] — [Referrer Name] mentioned I should reach out.

[One sentence about what you do and who you help.]

He/she said you're dealing with [specific problem referrer mentioned]. 

I've helped a few [company type] founders with exactly that. 

Worth a 20-minute call this week?
```

**Do not bury the referral name. Lead with it. Social proof is the opener.**

## Reply Handling Guide

**If they say "Yes, tell me more":**
Don't dump everything. Send one clear paragraph + a calendar link.
```
"Great. The simplest way to explain what I do is [1 sentence]. 

I'd rather show you directly than describe it — here's my calendar for a quick call: 
[Calendly link]. Pick whatever works."
```

**If they say "Not right now":**
```
"Totally fair. Mind if I check back in [30/60] days? Things change fast."
[If yes]: Add them to a follow-up reminder in your tracker.
```

**If they say "How much does it cost?":**
Never answer price in DMs. Move to a call.
```
"Depends on what you need — best covered in a quick call. Here's my calendar: 
[Calendly link]. 20 minutes, no pitch."
```

**If they go cold after expressing interest:**
```
"Hey [Name] — checking in. Still dealing with [their problem]?"
```
One sentence. Nothing more.

## Personalization Speed Hack

To personalize 10 DMs in under 30 minutes:

1. Open their LinkedIn profile
2. Copy their "About" section + most recent post into Claude or ChatGPT
3. Prompt: "Write a 1-sentence personalized observation I can use to open a cold DM to this person. I'm a strategy consultant who helps [ICP type] achieve [outcome]. Find something specific from their background or recent post — not generic flattery."
4. Paste the output into your DM template
5. Read it once to make sure it sounds human. Edit if needed.

**Do not skip personalization.** Generic DMs get 2–5% reply rates. Personalized DMs get 15–25%.
