# LinkedIn Content Templates

**Frequency:** 1–2 posts/week max (Monday required, Thursday optional)  
**Time investment:** 30–45 min/week total  
**Goal:** Build inbound pipeline on top of cold outreach — not replace it

---

## Template 1 — The Proof Post (Monday, Required)

This is your highest-converting post type. Post a variation of this every 2–3 weeks.

```
[Company type] was [painful before state].

[Specific problem that was causing it — 1 sentence].

Here's exactly what we changed:

1. [Specific tactic/decision — make it concrete]
2. [Specific tactic/decision]
3. [Specific tactic/decision]

The result: [specific measurable outcome] in [timeframe].

If you're a [ICP] dealing with [the same problem], the bottleneck 
is usually one of these three things.

DM me "SPRINT" and I'll show you which one applies to your situation.
```

## Template 2 — The Counterintuitive Take (Thursday, Optional)

No formatting. No list. Raw thought. These get 3–5x more organic reach than polished posts.

**Structure:** "[Common belief everyone has] is wrong. [Your counterintuitive truth]. [Why]. [What to do instead]. If this sounds backwards, you're probably [doing the thing everyone does wrong]."

**Examples:**

```
Most [company type] founders hire to fix their ops problems.

That's backwards.

Hiring before the process is defined just means you'll have more 
people doing the wrong thing faster.

Fix the system first. Then hire to run it.

The founders I work with who scale fastest hire last — not first.
```

```
The strategy document nobody reads isn't a strategy problem.

It's a format problem.

A 40-page deck gets reviewed once and filed. 

A one-page decision tree gets used every week.

If your team isn't using your strategic plan, the plan is wrong — 
not the team.
```

## Template 3 — The Case Study Hook (Use Every 3–4 Weeks)

```
I worked with a [ICP description] who was [specific painful situation].

They'd tried [what they'd already attempted — 2 things].

Neither worked.

When we dug in, the real problem wasn't [surface-level symptom].

It was [the actual root cause — the thing people miss].

Once we fixed that one thing, [specific outcome] happened in [timeframe].

The fix wasn't complicated. But it required [the key insight/move].

If you're in a similar spot, here's the question to ask yourself:

[One diagnostic question they can apply immediately]

Drop it in the comments or DM me if you want to talk through your situation.
```

## Posting Guidelines

**What to do:**
- Post at 7–9am or 12–1pm in your target audience's timezone (US Eastern = largest pool)
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours of posting (LinkedIn rewards engagement velocity)
- Comment on 3–5 posts by ICP accounts before and after posting (increases your post's reach)

**What to avoid:**
- Hashtag spam (max 3, relevant only)
- Engagement pods (LinkedIn detects and suppresses)
- Posting more than twice a week (diminishing returns + burns your posting hours)
- Deleting and reposting (resets engagement, doesn't help)

## Using AI to Write Posts

Paste this prompt into Claude:

```
I'm a strategy consultant who helps [ICP type] achieve [specific outcome].

My proof outcome: [your 200-word proof story].

Write a LinkedIn proof post in my voice using this structure:
- Before state (1 line)
- Root cause (1 line)
- What changed (3-item numbered list, specific and concrete)
- Result (1 line with numbers)
- CTA targeting [ICP] who have [their problem], asking them to DM me "SPRINT"

Tone: Direct. No corporate language. No emojis. No "I'm excited to share." 
Sound like a practitioner, not a thought leader.
```

Read the output. Edit anything that doesn't sound like you. Never post AI copy unedited.
