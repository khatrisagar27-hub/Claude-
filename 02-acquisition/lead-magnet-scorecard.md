## Score Interpretation Page

### Your Score: [Add up all 10 answers] / 50

**40–50: You're executing well.** Your bottleneck is likely scale or leverage. The next move is systematizing what's already working so it compounds without your constant input.

**25–39: You have the right instincts but inconsistent execution.** There's typically one foundational process that, when fixed, unlocks 3–4 other improvements downstream. This is exactly what I diagnose in the Strategy Sprint.

**10–24: You're building on unstable ground.** Trying to grow without fixing this first is like adding floors to a building with a cracked foundation. The Sprint starts here — diagnosing and stabilizing before scaling.

## Canva Design Tips

1. Use a Canva "Checklist" or "Workbook" template — don't design from scratch
2. Color scheme: 2 colors max (one dark, one accent)
3. Font: One sans-serif only (Inter, Lato, or Poppins)
4. Pages: Cover + Instructions + 10 questions (can be 2–3 per page) + Score page + CTA
5. Export as PDF Standard (not print quality — smaller file size)

**Canva template search terms:** "business workbook", "client assessment worksheet", "strategy scorecard"
