## Buying Signal (What Problem Are They Posting About?)

Your ICP is easiest to find when they're publicly talking about their pain. Look for:

- [ ] Posts about team/hiring frustrations
- [ ] Posts about missed revenue targets
- [ ] Posts about operational chaos
- [ ] Posts asking for recommendations on [your domain]
- [ ] Comments on competitors' content about [your topic]
- [ ] Job listings for roles you could help them avoid hiring for

## LinkedIn Search Setup

Use these filters in LinkedIn's People search (free account):

1. Go to LinkedIn Search → People
2. Filters to set:
   - **Current Company → Company Size:** Select your size range
   - **Title:** Type your target title(s) — use OR between options
   - **Industry:** Select your niche
   - **Geography:** Select your target countries/regions

## ICP Qualification Checklist (Before Sending DM)

Before DM'ing anyone, check:
- [ ] Their title matches your ICP
- [ ] Their company size is in your range
- [ ] Their industry is one where your proof is relevant
- [ ] They've posted in the last 60 days (active account)
- [ ] They have at least one post or comment you can reference personally

If they fail 2+ checks, skip them. There are unlimited targets. Quality of targeting = quality of replies.

---

## Anti-ICP (Do Not Target)

These waste your time. Add your own as you learn:

- [ ] Solo freelancers (no budget)
- [ ] Enterprise (>1,000 employees) — slow decisions, require procurement
- [ ] Non-English-speaking markets (until you've validated your offer in English first)
- [ ] People with zero LinkedIn activity (they won't see your DM)
- [ ] Anyone whose profile says "looking for opportunities" (not a decision-maker)
