The firm

About the practice

Sagar Khatri & Associates is a firm of Chartered Accountants established in 2022, founded and led by its proprietor, CA Sagar Khatri.

Based in Vadodara, Gujarat, the firm serves individuals, professionals and small and medium enterprises with audit and assurance, accounting, direct and indirect taxation, corporate law compliance and financial advisory.

The firm conducts its practice in accordance with the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and the standards and guidelines issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

Practice areas

Scope of work

Select an area to see what it covers.

Statutory audit

Audit of financial statements required by law, and the reports that accompany it.

  • Audit of the financial statements of companies under the Companies Act, 2013
  • Reporting under the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020, where it applies
  • Reporting on internal financial controls with reference to financial statements, where it applies
  • Audit of LLPs, trusts, societies and other entities under the laws that govern them
  • Tax audit under the income-tax law
  • Other attest and assurance engagements

Internal audit

Periodic review of processes and controls, reported to management and the Audit Committee.

  • Internal audit of companies under Section 138 of the Companies Act, 2013
  • Risk-based audit planning and scoping
  • Process reviews: purchase to payment, order to cash, inventory, payroll and treasury
  • Testing of internal financial controls
  • Reporting of observations and follow-up of agreed actions

Not undertaken for a company whose statutory audit the firm conducts (Section 144, Companies Act, 2013).

Accounting and book-keeping

Maintenance of books of account and preparation of periodic financial information.

  • Writing up and maintenance of books of account
  • Bank, vendor and customer reconciliations
  • Period-end closing and management reports
  • Preparation of financial statements in the formats prescribed by Schedule III to the Companies Act, 2013

Not undertaken for a company whose statutory audit the firm conducts (Section 144, Companies Act, 2013).

Goods and Services Tax

Registration, periodic returns and reconciliations under the GST law.

  • Registration, amendment and cancellation
  • Periodic returns in GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B
  • Annual return in GSTR-9 and reconciliation statement in GSTR-9C
  • Reconciliation of input tax credit with GSTR-2B
  • E-invoicing and e-way bill compliance
  • Refund applications and replies to notices
  • Support during GST audit, and advisory

TDS and TCS

Tax deducted and collected at source: computation, deposit and quarterly statements.

  • Review of deduction and collection obligations
  • Computation of monthly deposits
  • Quarterly TDS and TCS statements, and correction statements
  • Issue of TDS certificates
  • Reconciliation with the tax credit statement and resolution of defaults raised on TRACES
  • Applications for deduction at a lower or nil rate

Company law and ROC

Filings with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act, 2013 and the LLP Act, 2008.

  • Incorporation of companies and LLPs
  • Annual filings: AOC-4, MGT-7 and MGT-7A for companies; Form 8 and Form 11 for LLPs
  • DIR-3 KYC, ADT-1, DPT-3 and MSME-1
  • Event-based filings for changes in directors, share capital, registered office and charges
  • Statutory registers, minutes and secretarial compliance support

Income-tax

Returns of income, advance tax and proceedings before the income-tax authorities.

  • Returns of income for individuals, HUFs, firms, LLPs, companies and trusts
  • Tax planning and computation of advance tax
  • Reports and certificates required under the income-tax law
  • Replies to intimations and notices
  • Representation in assessment and appellate proceedings

Financial advisory

Business advisory, projections, and assistance with finance and regulatory matters.

  • Business advisory
  • Financial projections
  • Assistance with finance and regulatory matters
